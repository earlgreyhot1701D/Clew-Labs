§ 02 · Operating philosophy

Structure earns trust.

Trust is not a tone of voice layered over a product. It is the result of assigning evidence, calculation, uncertainty, and authority to the right parts of the system.

01

Evidence before conclusion

Consequential claims remain connected to their sources. Retrieval and inference are not treated as the same thing.

02

Structure before intelligence

Rules, boundaries, failure behavior, and deterministic calculations belong in the architecture before a model is invited in.

03

Human authority remains visible

AI can retrieve, extract, explain, and prepare. It should not quietly acquire the power to decide.

Unknown is a status, not a verdict.

§ 04 · About

The courtroom came first.

The code is newer. The systems judgment is not.

La Shara Cordero

I spent over ten years inside court operations and government before I began building software. I started this journey with a simple philosophy: learn by doing. Rather than analyzing AI from a distance, I began building tools in 2025 to test my questions through working systems. That experience shaped the questions I bring to technology: Who has authority? What evidence supports the claim? What happens when the data is incomplete? Who carries the consequence when an implementation fails?

I began building AI tools in 2025 to test those questions through working systems rather than presentations alone. Since then, I have shipped across civic technology, agent readiness, developer tools, education, and public service.

I build through a directed, review intensive AI workflow. I define the problem, architecture, constraints, acceptance criteria, and design. AI accelerates implementation. I inspect, test, and approve the result.
