§ 02 · Operating philosophy

Structure earns trust.

Trust is not a tone of voice layered over a product. It is the result of assigning evidence, calculation, uncertainty, and authority to the right parts of the system.

01

Evidence before conclusion

Consequential claims remain connected to their sources. Retrieval and inference are not treated as the same thing.

02

Structure before intelligence

Rules, boundaries, failure behavior, and deterministic calculations belong in the architecture before a model is invited in.

03

Human authority remains visible

AI can retrieve, extract, explain, and prepare. It should not quietly acquire the power to decide.

Unknown is a status, not a verdict.

La Shara Cordero

I spent over a decade inside court operations and government before I began building software. Clew Labs is my independent, woman-led laboratory for testing what responsible AI looks like when the consequences are real. I build to investigate: turning questions about authority, evidence, uncertainty, and public systems into working software that can be inspected.

“Who has authority? What evidence supports the claim? What happens when the data is incomplete? Who carries the consequence when an implementation fails?”

— The four inquiries governing every Clew Labs system

I build through a directed, review-intensive AI workflow: I define the problem, architecture, boundary constraints, acceptance criteria, and design. AI accelerates implementation. I inspect, challenge, and verify the result.
